Jobic is a modular CMS for building and running websites with your own content, design, and business rules. Give your team an editorial workspace and your developers a Laravel application they can extend.

Inside Jobic

One workspace for the people who build and run your site.

Give every kind of content its own structure.

Manage pages and posts with a block editor, language-specific drafts, previews, and revision history. Organize content with taxonomies and keep SEO fields with the entry.

Content editorIllustration
  • Title, excerpt, and SEO
  • Block-based body
  • Preview and revisions

Laravel at the core

Your Laravel skills come with you.

Use Blade for views, Eloquent for data, and policies for access. When your site needs a new integration or a business rule, build it with the same Laravel tools your team already knows.

About JobMetric

Built with an open-source ecosystem.

JobMetric builds reusable Laravel packages for application development. Jobic brings them together in a CMS, and developers can use the same packages in their own projects.
